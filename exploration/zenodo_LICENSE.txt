STATEMENT OF MODIFICATIONS

The nine within-cycle series (I, V, T, Qc, Qd, t, Qdlin, Tdlin, discharge_dQdV), the six
summary series not carried by the v1.0 analysis set (IR, QCharge, Tavg, Tmax, Tmin,
chargetime) and the Vdlin voltage grid were extracted from the three raw .mat batch
structs and rewritten as HDF5 plus a CSV index.

  - Cells were resolved by channel_id and barcode, never by struct position, and keyed by
    upper-cased barcode.
  - Cycles of the five cells continued across batches were concatenated into one
    numbering, matching the analysis set.
  - Eight of the nine series were converted from float64 to float32; `t` was not.
  - Cycles excluded from the v1.0 analysis set are RETAINED and flagged
    (beyond_crossing, is_placeholder) rather than dropped.
  - Nine series-level validity flags were added, each with a stated threshold.

No value was masked, imputed, smoothed, interpolated or repaired. The raw .mat files were
opened read-only and never modified.
